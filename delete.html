Delete your account and data: ADHD Time Blindness Table

Developer: Catalina Fechita (Belgium) · Contact: catafechita@gmail.com

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open ADHD Time Blindness Table.
  2. Tap the gear icon (top right) to open Settings & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

This is done right away.

Option 2: by email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to catafechita@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Tell us: - the name you used in Family, - the names of your groups and, if you can, roughly when you joined.

Family accounts have no email or password, so these details help us find the right account. We may ask one simple question to check that the account is yours. We will delete it within 30 days at the latest and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your Family account (your name, user ID and access key).
  • All tasks you added to shared groups.
  • Any group where you are the only member, with all its tasks and invite codes.
  • You are removed from groups you share with others. The other members keep their own tasks.

What is not on our server

Your Schedule, Parked tasks and Shopping list are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. To remove them, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps.

What we keep

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion. Short-term server logs from our hosting provider (IP address, time of request) expire on their own.
