Privacy Policy: ADHD Time Blindness Table

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the Android app ADHD Time Blindness Table ("the app") uses, why, and what your rights are.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Catalina Fechita, an independent developer based in Belgium. Contact: catafechita@gmail.com

The short version

  • Your Schedule, Parked tasks and Shopping list stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • You only share data with us if you use Family. Then we store the name you type, your groups, and the shared tasks on our server, so your group can see them.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. No analytics or advertising tools are in the app.
  • You can delete your Family account and data at any time in the app: Settings & privacy → Delete my account and data.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Schedule tasks, durations, the day start time, Parked tasks and the Shopping list are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Android's own device backup may include app data if you turned that on in your Google account settings.

2. Data we store on our server (only if you use Family)

Data Why Who can see it
The name you enter (for example "Mama Elena") To show who is in a group Members of your groups
A random user ID and a secret access key made by the app To recognise your phone. There is no email or password Only the server. The key is stored encrypted on your phone
Groups you create or join (group name, members) To run shared lists Members of that group
Shared tasks (text, done/not done, who added it, dates) To sync the list between phones Members of that group
Invite codes To let someone join a group Anyone you give the code to. Codes work once and expire after 30 minutes

Please don't put sensitive information (for example health details) in shared tasks. Everyone in the group can read them.

Legal basis (GDPR): providing the Family feature you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Keeping the service safe, for example limiting repeated attempts, is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

3. Technical data

When your phone talks to our server, the hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request details (time, address requested). We use IP addresses briefly in memory to block abuse (too many attempts). The hosting provider may keep short-term server logs. We do not use this to build profiles.

4. Service providers (processors)

We use these companies to run the Family feature. They process data only on our behalf: - Render (Render Services, Inc.): server hosting, EU region (Frankfurt, Germany). - MongoDB Atlas (MongoDB, Inc.): database hosting, EU region (AWS Frankfurt, Germany, eu-central-1). - GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts this policy page.

The app is distributed by Google Play. Google processes data under its own privacy policy when you download or update the app.

Our server and database are located in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). Some of these providers are US companies. Where data may be accessed from outside the EU, they rely on legal safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. How long we keep data

  • Family account, groups and tasks: until you delete them (delete a task, leave a group, or delete your account).
  • When the last member leaves a group, the group and all its tasks are deleted.
  • Invite codes are removed automatically about one day after they expire.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed from the live database right away.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your data, and to data portability. To use these rights, email catafechita@gmail.com. We will answer within one month. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données): https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be

7. How to delete your data

  • In the app: Settings & privacy (gear icon) → Delete my account and data → confirm.
  • Without the app: see https://catafechita-ux.github.io/timeblindness-legal/delete

8. Children

The app is a general planning tool and is not designed for young children. Children under 13 should only use Family with a parent's permission.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Your access key is stored in the phone's encrypted storage. Group data is only given to members of that group.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the change is important, we will also say so in the app.
